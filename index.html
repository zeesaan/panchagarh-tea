Our Story

A taste of Panchagarh in every cup.

Panchagarh is one of Bangladesh's most beautiful tea-growing regions. Located in the northern part of the country, the region is known for its unique climate, fertile soil and beautiful tea gardens.

Panchagarh Tea brings this authentic taste directly from the tea-growing region to tea lovers.

  • Authentic tea from Panchagarh
  • Carefully selected leaves
  • Quality-focused processing
  • Fresh delivery across Bangladesh
