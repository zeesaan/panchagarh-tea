About Panchagarh Tea

Tea with a Story from the North

Panchagarh is one of Bangladesh's emerging tea-growing regions, located in the northern part of the country. The unique landscape, soil and climate create an excellent environment for tea cultivation.

Our goal is to bring the authentic taste of Panchagarh tea directly to tea lovers while supporting the local tea-growing community.

From Panchagarh

Grown in the Beautiful Tea Gardens of Panchagarh

The tea gardens of Panchagarh offer a unique landscape in Bangladesh. Rows of green tea plants stretch across the northern countryside, creating a beautiful environment for tea cultivation.

We believe every cup should carry the natural character and authentic taste of the region where it comes from.
