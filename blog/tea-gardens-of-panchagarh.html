Discover the Tea Gardens of Panchagarh

Exploring the landscape, tea gardens and growing tea culture of northern Bangladesh.

← Back to Blog

Panchagarh is located in the northern part of Bangladesh and has developed a distinctive identity as a tea-growing area. The region's landscape, open fields and cool winter climate create an interesting environment for tea cultivation.

Tea in Northern Bangladesh

Tea cultivation in Panchagarh has become an important part of the agricultural landscape. Tea gardens can be seen across different parts of the district, creating long stretches of green fields.

From Garden to Cup

Every cup of tea begins with the tea plant. Leaves are carefully cultivated, harvested and processed before becoming the tea that people enjoy at home.

At Panchagarh Tea, our goal is to celebrate the taste and identity of tea from this region while connecting customers with authentic tea.

The Beauty of Panchagarh

Beyond tea, Panchagarh is known for its natural beauty and northern landscape. The combination of agriculture, greenery and the surrounding environment gives the region a special character.

For tea lovers, visiting the tea-growing areas of Panchagarh offers an opportunity to understand where the journey of a cup of tea begins.
