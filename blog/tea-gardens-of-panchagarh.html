Discover the Tea Gardens of Panchagarh

Panchagarh is one of Bangladesh's newer tea-growing regions. Located in the northern part of the country, the district has developed a distinctive tea landscape, particularly around Tetulia.

Unlike the traditional tea-growing areas of Sylhet and Chattogram, much of Panchagarh's tea cultivation developed on plain land. Small tea growers and tea gardens have become an important part of the region's agricultural landscape.

Bangladesh Tea Research Institute research describes tea cultivation in northern Bangladesh as beginning around 2000, with Tetulia in Panchagarh becoming an important centre for small-holding tea cultivation.

Where Are the Tea Gardens in Panchagarh?

Tea cultivation is especially associated with Tetulia Upazila in Panchagarh District.

Tea gardens and small tea holdings can be found across the northern tea-growing landscape, with Tetulia playing a central role in the development of Panchagarh tea.

The area is also known for its open countryside, green agricultural fields and views toward the northern horizon.

Why Does Tea Grow Well in Panchagarh?

The natural environment of Panchagarh is one of the reasons tea cultivation developed successfully in the region.

Bangladesh Tea Research Institute research has identified soil and climatic conditions in the area as favourable for tea cultivation.

The region's sandy-loam soil and acidic soil conditions are among the characteristics that can support tea plants.

The Himalayan Foothill Environment

Panchagarh is often described in Bangladesh Tea Research Institute research as being at the foothills of the Himalayas. Much of the tea-growing area is plain land, creating a distinctive environment for tea cultivation.

The Beginning of Panchagarh Tea

The story of tea in Panchagarh is relatively recent compared with the historic tea gardens of Sylhet.

Bangladesh Tea Research Institute records the beginning of tea cultivation in northern Bangladesh around 2000.

Tea professionals and private initiatives helped promote small-holding tea cultivation around Tetulia. A joint initiative involving the Bangladesh Tea Board and Rajshahi Krishi Unnayan Bank was subsequently launched to support small-holding tea cultivation in northern Bangladesh.

Small Tea Growers of Panchagarh

One of the most interesting parts of the Panchagarh tea story is the role of small growers.

Tea cultivation in the region is not limited to large traditional estates. Small tea holdings have become an important part of the tea-growing landscape.

This has allowed tea cultivation to spread across agricultural areas and has created opportunities for local farmers to participate in the tea industry.

What Does a Panchagarh Tea Garden Look Like?

A Panchagarh tea garden can look very different depending on its location and size.

Visitors may see rows of tea bushes covering open farmland, small tea holdings beside rural roads and larger areas of organized tea cultivation.

The bright green colour of the tea fields creates a beautiful contrast with the surrounding countryside.

Tea Leaves and Tea Plucking

Tea production begins with the careful management of tea bushes. Fresh shoots are periodically harvested and taken for processing.

The quality of the harvested leaves, cultivation practices, weather and processing methods all influence the final tea.

In Panchagarh, tea growers use a combination of agricultural knowledge and modern cultivation practices to maintain their gardens.

Tea Gardens and the Beauty of Tetulia

Tetulia is one of the most interesting places to experience Panchagarh's tea landscape.

The tea gardens are surrounded by the wider natural environment of northern Panchagarh, including rural roads, agricultural land and the Mahananda River area.

On clear days, the northern horizon can also offer beautiful views toward the Himalayan region.

Tea Gardens and Kanchenjunga

Panchagarh is famous among Bangladeshi travellers for occasional views of Kanchenjunga from the northern part of the district, particularly around Tetulia.

Clear weather and atmospheric conditions are important for distant mountain visibility.

This combination of tea gardens and the Himalayan horizon gives the Tetulia area a distinctive travel experience.

Tea Tourism in Panchagarh

Tea gardens are becoming an important part of the tourism identity of Panchagarh.

A visitor can combine a tea garden trip with several other destinations around the district.

  • Explore tea gardens around Tetulia.
  • Visit the Mahananda River area.
  • Explore Tetulia Dak Bungalow.
  • Continue toward Banglabandha.
  • Look for clear-day Himalayan views.
  • Explore Panchagarh's archaeological and historical sites.

Best Time to Explore the Tea Gardens

The cooler months are generally comfortable for exploring Panchagarh's outdoor attractions.

The winter season can be particularly pleasant for travelling around Tetulia and visiting tea-growing areas.

If you are specifically interested in seeing fresh green tea fields, conditions can vary according to the season, rainfall and local agricultural activities.

How to Visit the Tea Gardens

Tetulia is a useful starting point for exploring Panchagarh's tea-growing landscape.

Visitors travelling from Panchagarh town can continue toward Tetulia by road and explore the surrounding tea-growing areas.

Because individual tea gardens and small holdings may have different access arrangements, visitors should respect local property and ask permission before entering private tea gardens.

Tea Gardens and Local Communities

The development of tea cultivation has become connected with the local economy of northern Bangladesh.

Small tea growers, workers, processors and businesses all form part of the wider tea economy.

The growth of tea has therefore changed not only the agricultural landscape but also the economic identity of parts of Panchagarh.

Panchagarh's Growing Tea Industry

Bangladesh Tea Board continues to publish statistics on tea gardens and small-holding tea cultivation.

The Tea Board has also reported the completion of tea extension projects in Panchagarh and Lalmonirhat in 2023, supporting the continued development of northern Bangladesh's tea industry.

Panchagarh now has an established role in Bangladesh's broader tea sector.

The Panchagarh Tea Auction Centre

The development of a tea auction centre in Panchagarh added another important part to the region's tea industry.

The Panchagarh Tea Auction Centre provides a local platform for tea trading and reflects the growing importance of the northern tea-producing region.

Bangladesh Tea Board currently lists Panchagarh alongside Chattogram and Sreemangal as tea auction centres.

Why Visit a Panchagarh Tea Garden?

A visit to a Panchagarh tea garden is not only about seeing rows of green tea bushes.

It is an opportunity to understand how a relatively new tea region developed in Bangladesh and how tea became connected with the landscape and communities of northern Panchagarh.

A Different Tea Experience

Panchagarh offers a different perspective on Bangladeshi tea: plain-land tea cultivation, small growers, rural landscapes, Tetulia and the northern horizon all come together in one region.

Enjoy Authentic Panchagarh Tea

The story of Panchagarh tea is a story of land, farmers, cultivation and a growing regional identity.

From the first small tea holdings around Tetulia to today's expanding tea industry, Panchagarh has created an important new chapter in the history of Bangladeshi tea.

When you visit the tea gardens, take a moment to enjoy the landscape and experience the place where Panchagarh tea begins.

And after exploring the gardens, enjoy a cup of authentic Panchagarh Tea.
