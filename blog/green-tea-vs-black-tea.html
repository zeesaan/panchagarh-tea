Green Tea vs Black Tea

Understanding the differences in processing, taste, color and brewing.

← Back to Blog

Green tea and black tea both come from the same tea plant, but the processing methods are different. This creates differences in color, aroma and taste.

Green Tea

Green tea is processed to limit oxidation. It commonly has a lighter color and a fresh, grassy or vegetal character.

Black Tea

Black tea undergoes more oxidation during processing, creating a darker color and a deeper, richer flavor.

The Main Difference

The biggest difference is the way the tea leaves are processed after harvesting. Processing affects the appearance, aroma and flavor of the finished tea.

Which One Should You Choose?

There is no single choice that is right for everyone. Some people enjoy the fresh and lighter character of green tea, while others prefer the stronger and richer taste of black tea.

The best approach is to try both and discover which flavor profile you enjoy most.
