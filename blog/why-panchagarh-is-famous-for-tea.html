Why Panchagarh Is Special for Tea

Exploring the landscape, agriculture and growing tea identity of Panchagarh.

← Back to Blog

Panchagarh is located in the northernmost part of Bangladesh and has developed a strong connection with tea cultivation. Its tea gardens have become an important part of the local agricultural landscape.

A Different Tea-Growing Region

Tea cultivation in Panchagarh gives Bangladesh another important tea-growing landscape outside the country's traditional tea regions. The green fields create a distinctive northern tea environment.

The Landscape

The northern landscape of Panchagarh includes agricultural fields, greenery and open spaces. Tea gardens have become an increasingly recognizable part of this scenery.

Panchagarh Tea

Our goal is to share the taste and story of tea from this region with tea lovers across Bangladesh.

From Panchagarh to Your Cup

A cup of tea represents more than the finished drink. It represents the growers, gardens, processing and care behind the product.

Panchagarh Tea celebrates that journey and the growing tea identity of northern Bangladesh.
