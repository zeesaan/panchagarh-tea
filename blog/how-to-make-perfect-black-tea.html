How to Make the Perfect Cup of Black Tea

Simple steps for brewing a rich, aromatic and refreshing cup of black tea.

← Back to Blog

Making a great cup of black tea does not need to be complicated. The quality of the tea, water and brewing time all contribute to the final taste.

1. Start With Fresh Water

Fresh, clean water helps tea develop a better aroma and flavor. If possible, use freshly drawn water rather than water that has already been boiled several times.

2. Use the Right Amount of Tea

For a regular cup, start with approximately one teaspoon of loose black tea. You can adjust the amount according to your preferred strength.

3. Allow the Tea to Steep

Pour hot water over the tea leaves and allow them to steep for approximately three to five minutes. A shorter time produces a lighter cup, while a longer steep can produce a stronger flavor.

Tea Tip:

Do not rush the brewing process. Give the tea enough time to release its aroma and flavor.

4. Enjoy It Your Way

Black tea can be enjoyed plain or with milk and sugar depending on personal preference. Try different brewing times until you discover the flavor you enjoy most.
