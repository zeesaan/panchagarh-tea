The Story of Tea in Bangladesh

A look at tea cultivation, tea culture and the development of tea-growing regions in Bangladesh.

← Back to Blog

Tea has become an important part of Bangladesh's agricultural landscape and everyday culture. Tea is enjoyed throughout the country, from morning cups at home to traditional tea stalls and family gatherings.

Tea Cultivation in Bangladesh

Bangladesh has several tea-growing areas, with the Sylhet region being historically associated with tea cultivation. Over time, tea cultivation has expanded into other parts of the country.

The Rise of Panchagarh Tea

Panchagarh has developed into a notable tea-growing region in northern Bangladesh. Tea gardens have become part of the landscape and contribute to the region's agricultural identity.

Tea as Part of Daily Life

Tea is deeply connected with everyday social life in Bangladesh. A cup of tea can accompany conversations, work breaks, family gatherings and quiet moments.

At Panchagarh Tea, we are proud to be part of the continuing story of tea in Bangladesh.
