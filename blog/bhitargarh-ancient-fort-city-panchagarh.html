Bhitargarh Ancient Fort City

Explore one of Panchagarh's most important archaeological sites and discover the history hidden beneath the landscape of northern Bangladesh.

What Is Bhitargarh?

Bhitargarh is an ancient fortified archaeological site in Panchagarh, Bangladesh. It is located in Amarkhana Union under Panchagarh Sadar Upazila and is known for the remains of a large fortified settlement.

Archaeological research has identified multiple defensive enclosures, ramparts, moats and structural remains at the site. The settlement covers a very large area and provides an important window into the ancient history of the Panchagarh region.

Bhitargarh is especially interesting because its archaeological landscape includes four concentric fortified enclosures surrounded by earth and brick ramparts, with moats associated with the outer enclosures.

Where Is Bhitargarh Located?

Bhitargarh is located in Amarkhana Union of Panchagarh Sadar Upazila, Panchagarh District. The archaeological site is approximately 15–16 kilometres northeast of Panchagarh town, according to archaeological research and related documentation.

Its location in the northern part of Bangladesh also places it close to the historic and geographic routes connecting the Bengal region with areas farther north.

The Fortified Settlement of Bhitargarh

One of the most remarkable features of Bhitargarh is its fortification system. Archaeological investigations have identified four concentric quadrangles created by earth and brick ramparts.

These enclosures were separated from one another and protected by defensive structures. Moats were also associated with the fortification system, adding another layer of protection around the settlement.

The remains provide evidence of organized settlement planning and defensive construction in the region.

Archaeological Discoveries at Bhitargarh

Systematic archaeological investigation at Bhitargarh began in 2008 under the Center for Archaeological Studies at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh.

Research has documented the remains of ramparts, moats, foundations of religious and secular structures, irrigation features and different archaeological artifacts.

These discoveries have helped researchers understand more about the settlement pattern, construction methods and cultural landscape of ancient Bhitargarh.

Why Is Bhitargarh Important?

Bhitargarh is important because it represents a large fortified settlement with a complex layout. Its archaeological remains can help researchers study how people organized settlements, protected important areas and used the surrounding landscape in earlier periods.

The site is also significant for understanding the wider historical development of Panchagarh and northern Bangladesh.

Bhitargarh and Maharajar Dighi

Maharajar Dighi is another important historical feature associated with the Bhitargarh area. It is a large water body located within the wider archaeological landscape.

The combination of fortified structures, water management features and archaeological remains makes the Bhitargarh area particularly interesting for visitors interested in history and heritage.

A Place for History and Archaeology Lovers

Bhitargarh is different from many popular tourist destinations because much of its value comes from the landscape itself and the archaeological remains found there.

Visitors interested in ancient settlements, archaeology and the history of northern Bangladesh may find the site especially meaningful.

  • Explore the remains of ancient fortifications.
  • Learn about archaeological research in Panchagarh.
  • See the landscape around the historic settlement.
  • Combine the visit with other Panchagarh heritage destinations.
  • Experience the quieter side of northern Bangladesh.

How to Reach Bhitargarh

Bhitargarh can be visited from Panchagarh town by road. The site is in Amarkhana Union, northeast of the district town.

If you are planning a broader Panchagarh trip, Bhitargarh can be combined with other destinations such as Maharajar Dighi, the Rocks Museum, Tetulia and the Panchagarh tea gardens.

Best Time to Explore Bhitargarh

A comfortable season for exploring Panchagarh is generally the cooler period of the year. The dry and relatively pleasant weather can make outdoor heritage exploration easier.

Before travelling, it is a good idea to check local conditions and access information, especially during periods of heavy rain.

Bhitargarh and Panchagarh Tea

Panchagarh is known not only for its archaeological heritage but also for its tea gardens. A trip around the district can therefore combine history, nature and tea culture.

After exploring Bhitargarh, visitors can continue their journey toward the tea-growing areas of Panchagarh and Tetulia.

A cup of locally produced Panchagarh tea can be a simple way to end a day of exploring the region.

Explore More of Panchagarh

Bhitargarh is only one part of Panchagarh's heritage and natural landscape. You can continue exploring the district through its tea gardens, rivers, historical places and northern border destinations.
