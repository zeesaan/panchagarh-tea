Banglabandha Zero Point Panchagarh

Discover one of the most distinctive places in northern Bangladesh, where the country's northern boundary meets the landscape of Tetulia.

Panchagarh Tea Journal   •   September 24, 2026

Banglabandha Zero Point

Banglabandha Zero Point is one of the well-known destinations in Panchagarh, located in Banglabandha Union of Tetulia Upazila. It is recognized as the northernmost point of Bangladesh and is situated close to the international border with India.

The area is also associated with Banglabandha Land Port and the northern landscape of Tetulia. For visitors exploring Panchagarh, Banglabandha offers a different experience from the district's tea gardens, rivers and historical sites.

Quick fact: Banglabandha Zero Point is located in Banglabandha Union, Tetulia Upazila, Panchagarh, near the northern international boundary of Bangladesh.

Where Is Banglabandha Zero Point?

Banglabandha Zero Point is located in Tetulia Upazila of Panchagarh District. The Panchagarh District administration identifies Banglabandha Zero Point as a tourist spot in Banglabandha, Tetulia, Panchagarh.

The location is close to the Mahananda River and the India-Bangladesh border. Banglabandha Land Port is also located in the same border area.

Why Is Banglabandha Zero Point Special?

What makes Banglabandha interesting is its location. It represents the northern edge of Bangladesh and sits in an area where the country's border landscape, international trade and natural scenery come together.

The surrounding Tetulia region is also known for its tea gardens. This makes Banglabandha a natural addition to a Panchagarh trip that includes tea gardens and other attractions in the northern part of the district.

Banglabandha Land Port

Banglabandha Zero Point is closely connected with Banglabandha Land Port. The port is located in Tetulia Upazila of Panchagarh, opposite the Fulbari area of Jalpaiguri District in West Bengal, India.

According to Bangladesh Trade Portal information, Banglabandha Land Port was declared a land port in 2002 to facilitate import and export through the land route. The port has facilities for handling cargo and supporting border trade.

Banglabandha Land Port

  • Bangladesh side: Tetulia, Panchagarh
  • Opposite side: Fulbari, Jalpaiguri, West Bengal, India
  • Area: Banglabandha border area
  • Connection: International land trade route

What Can You See Around Banglabandha?

The Banglabandha area has a different character from the busy urban areas of Bangladesh. The landscape around Tetulia includes open countryside, tea gardens, border areas and the Mahananda River.

On a clear day, visitors can enjoy the wider northern landscape while exploring the area around Banglabandha and Tetulia.

Banglabandha and the Mahananda River

The Mahananda River is an important natural feature of the Banglabandha area. The river runs through the northern landscape of Tetulia and adds another interesting destination for visitors exploring this part of Panchagarh.

The combination of the river, border landscape and nearby tea gardens gives the northern part of Panchagarh a character that is different from many other parts of Bangladesh.

How to Travel to Banglabandha Zero Point

Visitors can first travel to Panchagarh and then continue toward Tetulia and Banglabandha by local transportation, private car or other suitable road transport.

The Panchagarh District government tourism information states that visitors arriving in Panchagarh can travel approximately 55 km toward Banglabandha using a local bus, private vehicle or microbus.

Simple Travel Route

  • Dhaka → Panchagarh
  • Panchagarh → Tetulia
  • Tetulia → Banglabandha
  • Banglabandha → Zero Point area

Best Time to Explore Banglabandha

Banglabandha can be included in a Panchagarh trip throughout the year, subject to local conditions and border-area restrictions.

Winter is particularly popular for tourism in Panchagarh because visitors often combine trips to Banglabandha with Tetulia, tea gardens and views toward the Himalayan region.

If you are planning a visit, it is a good idea to check local travel conditions and any restrictions that may apply around the international border before starting your journey.

Other Places to Visit in Panchagarh

Banglabandha is only one of many places that can be included in a Panchagarh trip. Visitors can combine it with tea gardens, historical places, rivers and other attractions around the district.

Some places worth exploring include Tetulia, the Mahananda River, Bhitargarh, Maharajar Dighi, Rocks Museum and the tea gardens of Panchagarh.

Banglabandha and Panchagarh Tea

Panchagarh is well known for its tea-growing landscape, especially around Tetulia. The journey toward Banglabandha passes through an area where visitors can experience the unique combination of northern Bangladesh's countryside and tea gardens.

For tea lovers, a trip to Panchagarh is more than simply visiting a destination. It is an opportunity to see where tea is grown, experience the landscape and learn more about the tea culture of northern Bangladesh.

A Journey to the Northern Edge of Bangladesh

Banglabandha Zero Point offers a memorable experience because of its geographical location and connection to the border landscape. Together with the tea gardens and other attractions of Tetulia, it can become an interesting part of a Panchagarh travel itinerary.

Whether you are interested in geography, travel, tea gardens or the landscapes of northern Bangladesh, Banglabandha is a place worth learning about when exploring Panchagarh.

Explore Panchagarh: After visiting Banglabandha, take time to explore the tea gardens and natural beauty of Tetulia and discover the unique tea-growing region of Panchagarh.
